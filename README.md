# AI playbooks

Prompts and guides from my **I Asked an Agent** series. Practical things you can ask an AI agent to build or do for your own use.

## Photo albums

Turn photographs of old album pages into individual pictures you can browse and share. Ask an agent to build a local app with cropping, perspective correction, rotation, face detection, glare checks and saved batch progress.

[Get started](photo-albums/README.md) · [Full agent prompt](photo-albums/prompt.md)

## Career evidence

Build a durable record of your career through a systematic interview. The agent helps recover forgotten or overlooked experience, preserves facts and uncertainty, and records how the evidence could support future CVs, resumes, applications and interviews.

[Get started](career-evidence/README.md) · [Full agent prompt](career-evidence/prompt.md)

## Recurring spending

Bring bank statements and supporting payment records together to identify recurring spending. Ask an agent to build a local review with payment histories, merchant research, saved notes and updates when you add more statements.

[Get started](recurring-spending/README.md) · [Full agent prompt](recurring-spending/prompt.md)

Each playbook has a short guide for you and a complete prompt to give your agent.
